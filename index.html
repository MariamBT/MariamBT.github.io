Knotz

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy explains how Knotz handles personal information when you use the Knotz iOS app and its supporting services. It should be read with the Terms of Service.

Information you provide

We process your account email address, display name, username, profile photo, and any content you choose to add to Knotz. That content can include knots, messages, chat photos, polls and votes, tasks, links, attendance, saved locations, wishlist items, and an optional shipping address. If you contact us, we also receive the email address and message content you choose to send.

Information created through use

We process technical information needed to operate the app, such as authentication session information, security and error records, and a push-notification token if you allow notifications. Knotz does not ask for a precise device location; any location name, address, or map link is information you choose to save.

How information is used

We use this information to create and secure accounts; show your profile and shared content to the people in your knots; deliver chat, tasks, polls, and notifications; maintain the service; and respond to support and safety issues. We do not sell personal information.

Who can see shared information

Your display name, username, profile photo, and activity in a knot can be visible to other members of that knot. Information in a shared wishlist can be visible to people you choose. Please do not post personal or sensitive information you would not want other members to see.

Service providers

Knotz uses Supabase to provide authentication, database, and file-storage services; Expo services for push notifications; and Google Mobile Ads for iOS banner advertising. These providers process data only as needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy notices.

Advertising and tracking

Knotz displays iOS banner ads through Google Mobile Ads. Google may process device and app information under its own privacy policy to deliver, measure, and protect ads. Knotz does not currently present an iOS App Tracking Transparency permission prompt. Your device privacy settings and Google’s controls may affect the ads you receive.

Push notifications

If you allow notifications, Knotz stores a device token to send alerts about chat messages, tasks, and knot activity. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and shared-content records while an account is active. You can edit certain profile information in the app. To permanently delete your account, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account, read the consequences, type DELETE, and confirm. The deletion flow removes active account records and files, including personal content. Knots you own and their shared content are removed for members as well. Limited backup copies may remain temporarily for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or disaster recovery.

Children

Knotz is not for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, report the user in the app so we can review the account.

Your choices and questions

You may manage your profile, notification permission, and account deletion from the app. For privacy questions or requests, email knotzapp8@gmail.com. This address is also available from Profile → Settings → Support & ideas.
