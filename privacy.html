Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy explains how Knotz handles personal information when you use the Knotz iOS app and its supporting services. It should be read with the Terms of Service.

Information you provide

We process your account email address, display name, username, profile photo, and any content you choose to add to Knotz. That content can include knots, messages, chat photos, polls and votes, tasks, links, attendance, saved locations, wishlist items, and an optional shipping address. If you contact us, we also receive the email address and message content you choose to send.

Information created through use

We process technical information needed to operate the app, such as authentication session information, security and error records, and a push-notification token if you allow notifications. Knotz does not ask for a precise device location; any location name, address, or map link is information you choose to save.

How information is used

We use this information to create and secure accounts; show your profile and shared content to the people in your knots; deliver chat, tasks, polls, and notifications; maintain the service; and respond to support and safety issues. We do not sell personal information.

Who can see shared information

Other signed-in users may find your profile through people search, including your display name, username, and profile photo. Your activity and shared content in a knot can be visible to other members of that knot. Information in a shared wishlist can be visible to people you choose. Please do not post personal or sensitive information you would not want other members to see.

Service providers

Knotz uses Supabase to provide authentication, database, and file-storage services; Expo services for push notifications; and Google Mobile Ads for iOS banner advertising. These providers process data only as needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy notices.

Chat screening and safety reports

When chat screening is enabled, we ask for your permission before sending new chat text and photos to OpenAI for automated safety screening. OpenAI API content is not used for model training by default. Authorized Knotz moderators may review flagged submissions, reported content and account reports to enforce our rules. Reports include a server-captured copy of the content. Screening can make mistakes; contact support to appeal. You can decline or withdraw permission in Settings → Chat safety and continue reading chats, but cannot submit new messages or photos without screening permission. Withdrawal stops future screening, not processing already completed or in progress. We do not send existing conversations for retrospective screening. See https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data for OpenAI’s API data practices.

Safety evidence and retention

Pending chat photos are stored privately. Safety maintenance normally removes submission copies after 30 days, and closed content-report evidence 30 days after review, while keeping media still attached to a published message or an open report. Unsubmitted uploads become eligible for cleanup after one day. Open safety incidents require human resolution; material subject to a legal preservation requirement may need separate handling. Published chat content otherwise follows the account and content-deletion rules below. A support appeal can be sent using Settings → Support & ideas.

Shared text outside chat

When shared-text screening is enabled, we ask permission before sending new or edited shared names, bios, knot descriptions and location labels, task titles, polls and options, links, and wishlist text to OpenAI for automated safety screening. We do not include private shipping addresses in this check. OpenAI API content is not used for training by default. Declining prevents publishing that text but does not prevent reading. Permission lasts for the app session and can be withdrawn in Settings; processing already in progress cannot be recalled. Only approved text may be saved through the protected client paths. Flagged text is not published; contact support to appeal or request human review. Short-lived approval records include the submitted text and account identifier, expire after five minutes, and are removed after a successful save or by scheduled cleanup normally every ten minutes. Account deletion also removes them. This text check does not screen non-chat photos or inspect the destination content of links.

Advertising and tracking

Knotz is available to people aged 13 and older. Before using the app, everyone must declare an age group: 13–17 or 18 or older. This cannot be skipped. We store your stated age group with your account until account deletion, not your birth date. This is self-declared information, not independent age verification. Contact support if you need to correct it. People under 13 cannot continue. Teen accounts do not receive ads. Ad requests remain disabled until the signed-in account’s saved adult declaration is checked, and Google consent requirements still apply. When permitted, Knotz displays iOS banner ads through Google Mobile Ads and requests non-personalized ads with the strictest creative-content rating. Non-personalized ads can still involve device identifiers, IP addresses, and other device or app information for delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. Where required, Google’s consent form asks for your choices before ads are requested. Adult confirmation does not replace Google consent. You can revisit your choices through Settings → Ad privacy options when that option is required or available. Knotz does not currently present an iOS App Tracking Transparency permission prompt. Your device privacy settings and Google’s controls may affect the ads you receive.

Profile and knot photo screening

When photo screening is enabled, profile and knot images are resized and compressed on your device. Each upload asks permission to send the selected image to OpenAI for a safety check before it is made public. OpenAI API content is not used for training by default. Declining cancels the upload. Rejected images are not published; contact support to appeal. Approved images may be publicly accessible by their URL, so do not upload private or sensitive images. An approved image from an abandoned edit may remain until account deletion. Upload records support cleanup and are removed with the account; an interrupted upload may delay deletion until it is reconciled. Existing photos are not retrospectively screened.

Push notifications

If you allow notifications, Knotz stores a device token to send alerts about chat messages, tasks, and knot activity. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and shared-content records while an account is active. You can edit certain profile information in the app. To permanently delete your account, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account, read the consequences, type DELETE, and confirm. Once accepted, deletion continues on our servers even if you close the app. Acceptance is not immediate completion; temporary failures are retried. Knots you own and their shared content are removed for members as well, including associated chat photos unless another member’s photo is still referenced in a surviving knot. Other members’ accounts are not deleted. A restricted operational record of the deleted account identifier, request and completion times, and retry information is retained to track processing and prevent duplicate work. Completed records become eligible for automatic daily cleanup 30 days after completion. Unfinished jobs and records with unresolved account or file cleanup are retained until resolved. Contact support for deletion-status questions. Limited backup copies may remain temporarily for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or disaster recovery.

Children

Knotz is not for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, report the user in the app so we can review the account.
